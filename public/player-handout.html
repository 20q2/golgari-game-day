The Shadow War

Twelve days until the Long Dark reaches our walls. His three lieutenants are already inside the borders.

Each Day

the loop

Every one of you takes one action. You all declare before anyone rolls. Then the Colossus gets its order, and the Standing Army gets its own. Then the night event is resolved.

Resolving an action

  • 10+Success — 1 point
  • 25+Great Success — 2 points
  • 9 or lessFailure — nothing
  • nat 20Always a Great Success
  • nat 1Always a failure

Also true

  • PushAfter a failure, take one level of exhaustion and reroll
  • AssistGive up your action to grant someone advantage
  • StackTwo of you may take the same action; results add up
  • RestA long rest every night, unless something stops you

Using your character sheet

Flavour changes the story, not the roll. A well-suited spell or tool grants advantage. Something that outright solves the problem — scrying on a named lieutenant, planar ally spent on a negotiation — is an automatic Great Success. Once per resource; the same trick doesn't work twice.

War Map Actions

one per person, per day

Gather IntelStealth · Investigation

Three lieutenants serve the Shadow Lord. Each is uncovered in three stages, in order: their name, then a description and where they lair, then their secrets.

One point buys one stage.

Commission an ItemArcana · Artisan's tools

Build from the list of twenty-eight separate sheet. Standard items cost 1 point, so a Great Success is two copies. Major items cost 2 points: one Great Success, two of you pooling a success, or one of you banking today and finishing tomorrow.

The same action purifies a captured artifact. Three items cannot be built until you have Stage III intel on their target.

Fortify DefensesAthletics · Sleight of Hand · Religion / Arcana

Three tracks at Starbrook — Bulwark, Sky, Ward — five levels each. Name the track before you roll. One point raises it one level.

Each level is either passive (higher ground, unquenchable light, wards in the stone) or adds a garrison order you can command in battle. Each of the three lieutenants tests a different track at night.

Beseech AlliesPersuasion · Insight

Name one of the allied nations. On a success, choose one:

  • Their special power
  • 2 Muster
  • Push the shadow back out of their borders by one step

On a Great Success, take two different ones.

Other Actions

no check

Launch an Assault all four of you

Take the fight to a lieutenant whose name you know. One killed now is one absent from the last battle, and its artifact is yours.

If you go without knowing where they lair, you spend the day finding the place: you arrive with no short rest, you cannot spend Hit Dice first, and they act before you do.

Everything you have secured can be brought — allied powers, Muster, the Colossus and the Standing Army if they are standing at the lair.

Raise or Reinforce an Army War Roomno check · 1,000 gp

One day and a thousand gold raises a Standing Army — about a thousand riders and foot, at Strength 3. No roll.

One Standing Army at a time. If one is already in the field, this action reinforces it instead — +1 Strength for 1,000 gp, to a maximum of 3, as often as you like.

Buying Strength back always costs one of you a day. The army never spends one of its own orders on it, and it keeps marching, holding or striking while you do the paperwork.

The Colossus & the Standing Army

pieces on the map

The Colossus the party's

It has no hit points and cannot be destroyed. It stands in one region, and each day the four of you agree on one order:

  • MOVE — one region.
  • HOLD — its region gains no shadow tonight, from any source.
  • STRIKE — remove 1 shadow from its region; or, if it stands at a lair on an assault day, it joins the fight for 60 damage a round, ignoring resistance, and soaks a wave of minions each round.

In the final battle it fights the whole fight — if it is standing at Starbrook. Otherwise it arrives a round later for every region it has to cross.

The Standing Army its raiser's

Strength 3. Its orders are given by whoever raised it. At Strength 0 it is destroyed.

  • MARCH — one region.
  • HOLD THE LINE — prevent 1 shadow in its region tonight. Costs 1 Strength if it prevents anything.
  • STRIKE — remove 1 shadow from its region; or join an assault at the lair it stands in. Costs 1 Strength either way.

Strength is bought back with the Raise or Reinforce an Army action, not with one of these orders. An army does not resupply itself.

In a battle it adds a garrison order: Send in the Riders — 4d10 to each of up to three enemies in a 60-ft line, each making a DC 18 Str save or knocked prone with speed halved.

You may also disband it mid-battle as a free action for 2 Muster per remaining Strength.

In a Battle

what your preparations do

Garrison Orders

At the top of every round, the party commands one of the things you've built. It costs nobody a turn, action, or reaction.

Fire the cannons. Loose the ballistae. Throw the chains. Send in the riders.

One order a round, no matter how much you own. Some orders are once per battle.

Muster charges

Up to 2 charges per round, in any fight, no action required, any conscious one of you may call it. Charges are spent permanently; they do not refresh between fights.

  • Intercept — a soldier steps in front of an attack that has already hit. It misses instead. Works on anything with an attack roll, including legendary actions.
  • Field Medic — a character at 0 hit points is dragged clear, stabilised, and stands with half their hit point maximum. They lose no turn to it.
  • Haul Them Out — move an ally up to 30 ft to a space you choose and end one of grappled, restrained or prone on them.
  • Rally — one ally rerolls a failed saving throw against fear, charm, or psychic damage.
  • Break Ranks — one minion is removed from the field entirely. Not a lieutenant, and not the Shadow Lord.

No damage option, deliberately. Everything here does something you cannot do for yourselves.

Your Defences

five levels per track

Everything you build at Starbrook is either passive — simply true, all the time — or a garrison order you can command at the top of a round. Levels are earned in order, and every one is permanent once built.

BulwarkAthletics · Survival

  1. Fire the Cannons garrison
    Two guns. One or two targets within 600 ft: +12 to hit, 6d10 force each, hitting automatically against anything Huge or larger. Shadow creatures have no resistance to it.
  2. Terraform: the Slope passive
    Ground within 60 ft outside the walls is difficult terrain for enemies, and ranged attacks made from the walls get +2 to hit.
  3. Throw the Chains 1 per battle
    One Huge or larger creature within 120 ft is restrained and cannot teleport until the end of its next turn. No save.
  4. Watchfires passive
    The whole field is in bright light, and magic of 6th level or lower cannot dim it.
  5. Into the Deepwork 1 per battle
    You and anyone you name within 30 ft drop into the tunnels: total cover from one effect, however large. You re-emerge anywhere along the walls.

SkySleight of Hand · Perception

  1. Loose the Ballistae garrison
    Two bolts within 600 ft: +12 to hit, 4d10 piercing each, auto-hitting Huge or larger. A creature hit has its fly speed halved and cannot Disengage until the end of its next turn.
  2. Skywatch passive
    Nothing surprises Starbrook. You are never surprised at home, and every assault you launch opens with a surprise round — even if all you have is a name.
  3. Fog Nets passive
    Any flying creature above Starbrook has its speed halved and cannot Disengage.
  4. Signal 1 per battle
    Reroll any one d20 rolled by anyone on the field — or re-run an allied nation's power that has just misfired.
  5. Call the Rig 1 per battle
    The storm rig discharges: a 100-ft line, 16d6 lightning, DC 20 Dex for half. You choose the line and the moment.

WardReligion · Arcana

  1. Scour garrison
    The scopes rake a 20-ft radius within 600 ft: 5d8 radiant, no save, and every shadow creature in it loses concealment until the end of its next turn.
    Also passive: spells cast from the walls have double range.
  2. Consecration passive
    Your home region can no longer take shadow from spread — only from a direct strike. In a fight, a shadow or undead creature takes 3d6 radiant the first time each turn it comes within 30 ft of the walls.
  3. Dampen 1 per battle
    Negate one lair action or one legendary action as it is used, before it takes effect.
  4. Truesight Lattice passive
    Everyone on your side ignores shadow concealment, invisibility and illusion inside the field. Also +2 to every Gather Intel check for the rest of the war.
  5. Break the Sigil 1 per battle
    Until the end of the round, no creature on the field may use Legendary Resistance, and one magical effect of your choice ends.

Remember the cap: one garrison order a round, however many you own. Building deep buys you choices, not extra attacks.

eight of them
